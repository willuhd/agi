1What is wrong today

From the one real G11-AP week we have, and a catalog audit:

  • Students carry the walking. Every teacher owns a room, so a student changes rooms ~21×/week (488 s of walking on the observed week).
  • Slots are filled with no objective: stranded free periods between classes (2.8/week), hard subjects late in the day.
  • Period length is ignored. Periods run 60/40/40/45/55/40/40 min, so swapping a Math lesson from P1 to P2 silently removes 20 min — breaking the one rule the brief names: hours must not change. 110 of 270 sections drift out of band in a typical status-quo grid.
  • Nothing is verified and a room loss is repaired by hand.
Honest scope. We have one real week. The “current system” here is an emulator calibrated to it (~20 vs 21 observed room changes), run 24 times to get a spread.

3Method: four stages

0 Sectioning. Catalog rules → offered pathways; a pathway = one class with an identical timetable (kills cross-class clash cliques).
↓
1 Slots (CP-SAT). Boolean $z_{g,s}$ per section×slot — linear, not $O(M^2)$ pairwise.
↓
2 Rooms. Cohort home rooms → per-slot min-cost matching → exact person-seconds simulated annealing.
↓
3 Certify. A separate checker re-derives 10 rules from the finished grid; mutation tests prove it can fail.
↓
4 Repair. Substitute → re-room → re-slot only the affected sections, all else frozen.

4The mathematics

Loads: catalog credit → weekly slots

$$n_c=\Big\lfloor k\,\kappa_c+\tfrac12\Big\rfloor,\qquad k=\frac{\sum_i \kappa_i\,o_i}{\sum_i \kappa_i^{2}}\approx 0.873$$

$\kappa_c$ catalog credit, $o_i$ slots observed on the real week; swept $k\in[0.83,1]$.

Hard constraints (decision $z_{g,s}\in\{0,1\}$)

$$\sum_s z_{g,s}=n_g\quad \sum_{g\in\sigma} z_{g,s}\le 1\quad \sum_{g\in T_t} z_{g,s}\le 1\quad \sum_{g:\,a_g\ge a} z_{g,s}\le R_{\kappa}(a)$$

students $\sigma$, teacher $t$, rooms of kind $\kappa$ with area $\ge a$ (nested ⇒ Hall's condition is exact).

Contact-hours invariant (minutes, not slots)

$$\Big|\sum_s \mu_s z_{g,s}-n_g\bar\mu\Big|\le\max\big(\tau\, n_g\bar\mu,\;9\big),\qquad \tau=0.08$$

Stage-1 objective

$$\min\; w_{\text{gap}}\sum_\sigma c_\sigma\Gamma_\sigma + w_h\sum_\sigma c_\sigma H_\sigma + w_{sp}\sum_g |g|\,\text{rep}_g + w_m\sum_{g\,\text{hard}}|g|\sum_s (p_s-1)z_{g,s} + w_{tg}\sum_t\Gamma_t$$

$\Gamma$ idle gaps, $H$ days with ≥6 classes, rep same-day repeats, $c_\sigma$ students in signature $\sigma$.

Stage-2 objective: person-seconds

$$\min_{r}\;\sum_{(a,b)\in\mathcal L_{\text{stud}}} n_{ab}\,\tau(r_a,r_b)\;+\;\lambda\sum_{(a,b)\in\mathcal L_{\text{teach}}}\tau(r_a,r_b)$$

Composite score and fairness

$$J=\bar{\ell}_{\text{student}}+\alpha\,\overline{L}_t+\beta\,\mathrm{CVaR}_q(L),\qquad \mathrm{CVaR}_q=\tfrac{1}{\lfloor(1-q)n\rfloor}\sum_{\text{worst}}L_i$$

Robustness

$$\mathbb E[\Delta]=\sum_{\xi} p_\xi\,\Delta(\xi),\quad \Delta=\text{moved lessons}+\text{room changes}+\text{uncovered periods}$$
